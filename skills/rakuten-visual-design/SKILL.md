# Rakuten Visual Design

## Art direction

Create a polished Japanese ecommerce visual language that is clean, appealing, easy to scan, and appropriate to the product category. Do not apply one generic pastel or banner template to every product.

## Typography and layout

- Use Japanese-capable fonts. Prefer a coherent Japanese sans-serif family for modern product graphics; use Mincho only when the product's mood justifies it.
- Establish clear headline, subheadline, body, annotation, and specification levels.
- Avoid mixing too many typefaces, weights, outlines, shadows, and decorative effects.
- Keep text large enough for smartphone viewing. If copy is too long, propose a shorter version separately; do not silently alter locked copy.
- Align elements to a consistent grid with generous margins and clear negative space.
- Do not stack every line vertically or center every block by default.
- Ensure text contrast against the background.

## Suite consistency

Lock a Visual DNA sheet before production:
- Main and accent colors.
- Font family and weights.
- Type scale.
- Grid and safe margins.
- Background / lighting style.
- Icon and callout style.
- Border, corner, and shadow language.

Maintain consistent visual identity while varying composition according to each image's purpose. Avoid repeating the same hero scene across multiple images.

## Product compositing

Prefer the original product photo, careful cutout, proportional scaling, and compositing over AI regeneration. Use generated scenes as backgrounds or supporting context when that reduces product drift. Never stretch, warp, recolor, redraw, or fabricate product components.

## Image QA

Review each image for:
- Product shape, color, texture, print, count, packaging, and accessories.
- Japanese spelling and line breaks.
- Visual hierarchy and mobile readability.
- Alignment, spacing, crop, and safe margins.
- Scene plausibility, shadows, contact points, and perspective.
- Consistency with the approved suite.
- Whether this image communicates a distinct reason to buy.

A beautiful image that misrepresents the product fails QA.
