# First Pilot Brief — Crispy Squishy Decompression Toys

## Product positioning
Primary purchase appeal: decompression / satisfying tactile play. This is a sensory play toy, not a conventional problem-solving household product. Do not invent stress-relief efficacy or therapeutic claims.

## User-approved hero direction
- Main hero: butter-stick squishy is dominant and enlarged.
- Other SKUs: a few may appear as supporting variety, not competing focal points.
- Preserve exact source product. No AI redraw, shape changes, color changes, added accessories, or fabricated package contents.
- Remaining images are planned according to their individual roles.

## Three pilot images
### Image 1 — Hero
Objective: make the butter-stick squishy immediately recognizable and desirable.
Composition: enlarged butter-stick as primary subject; selected other SKUs smaller and secondary; clean Japanese ecommerce art direction and clear focal hierarchy.
Do not turn the product into real butter/food, change silhouette, invent crumbs/liquid/packaging, or let other SKUs dominate.

### Image 2 — Decompression experience
Objective: communicate the actual squeeze-and-play experience.
Composition: close crop of a real hand squeezing the exact toy, with texture visible. If deformation is not documented, use a non-deformed close-up and avoid claiming specific rebound or sound.
Proposed copy, not locked: 「ぎゅっと握って、気分転換。」 User approval required.

### Image 3 — Everyday use scene
Objective: help shoppers imagine everyday leisure use.
Composition: desk or home break setting; exact product visible in hand or beside the user; product remains focal.
Proposed copy, not locked: 「ちょっとした休憩時間のおともに」 User approval required.

## Source mapping
Map every visible SKU to its original source file before rendering. The previously discussed family includes butter-stick, chocolate, donut, cat-paw, and 12-piece assortment packs; confirm labels against source images rather than assuming A-J mapping.

## Acceptance
All three images must pass quality-pilot.md. Product identity, SKU mapping, and Japanese text are hard-fail checks.
